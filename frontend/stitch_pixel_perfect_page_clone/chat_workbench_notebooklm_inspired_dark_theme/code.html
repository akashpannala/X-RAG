What is our company remote work expense and equipment reimbursement policy, and who needs to approve high-value ergonomic items?

bolt
X-RAG Synthesizer • RAG Verified

Under the Global Remote Work Policy (revised Q1 2025), full-time employees are eligible for an initial $1,000 home office equipment stipend upon joining, plus a recurring $150/quarter reimbursement for utilities and high-speed internet .

For high-value equipment or ergonomic requests (exceeding $500 individually, such as motorized standing desks or ergonomic chairs), standard self-service submission is not permitted. Approval must follow a two-tier review:

  • check_circle Level 1: Direct People Manager approval via Workday
  • check_circle Level 2: Workplace Ergonomics & Operations Team review

All approved items must be ordered through the approved vendor portal to remain compliant with corporate asset tracking .
